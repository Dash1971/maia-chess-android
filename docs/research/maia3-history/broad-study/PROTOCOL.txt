Mobile Maia history study — stages 1–4
Registered 2026-10-09 JST before model outcomes on study samples.

Question: should Mobile Maia feed the released 79M checkpoint actual recent board history rather than repeat the current board? This study does not change the app or run a strength tournament.

Primary comparison: same pinned 79M ONNX (SHA256 3454b03ae78baa64a87b345fdb1a457265d912caec531039b074f07eda0d8010), same board and legal moves, actual self/opponent ratings. R = current board repeated eight times; H = seven prior boards plus current board, oldest first, each board oriented by its own side to move, earliest-board left padding, zero final ponder channel. Temperature 1 / TopP 1. Use exact upstream encoding, not an invented orientation variant. Compare with official PyTorch checkpoint on a small preflight sample; require identical input tensors and close legal probabilities. Include a Black-to-move, short history, and promotion case. No model cache shared across different histories.

Source: original yimingzhang/allie-data annotated 2022 blitz test games, revision 990f79105b1f08a8fd367fcb10c5e18c69436e30. Source predates published Maia training period 2023-01–2025-07. Reconstruct all moves; reject invalid games and duplicate game IDs/sequences. Record eligibility/attrition. Only players with both ratings 600–2600 inclusive; require standard start. Accumulate recorded move-seconds and increments to reconstruct clocks. Stop eligibility at the first pre-move or pre-increment post-think clock below 30 seconds for either player; this conservative filter is our study convention, not a claim of exact paper replication. Reject invalid durations. Do not feed future moves into history.

Split games by stable SHA256 hash: first 5% to pilot, rest to main. Seed 2026100901. Pilot: 1,000 nonopening positions to measure throughput, validate all metrics and detect pipeline failures; never pool with main. Main target 20,160 observations: 840 per rating-band x color x stage cell (4 x 2 x 3). Rating bands 600–1199, 1200–1599, 1600–1999, 2000–2600. Stages moves 11–20, 21–40, 41+. Cap sampled candidates at two per game/color/stage. Select before inference with deterministic random priorities. If a cell is short, use all its eligible observations and report shortage without replacement, duplication, or borrowing after observing results. Analyze the designed balanced sample, not as population-weighted estimates.

Primary metric: paired change H minus R in human-move negative log likelihood (natural logs; lower is better). Secondary: top-one accuracy, mean probability of played move (expected match under raw sampling), multiclass Brier score, total variation between policies, probability assigned to legal mirrored copy of opponent's last move and immediate reversal of mover's last move. Define copy by vertical square reflection of immediately preceding UCI move; only score when legal; define reversal as current move's from/to equal mover's previous to/from, without promotions. Symmetry is not itself an error. Report corresponding actual human frequencies. Stratify by rating, color, stage; intervals are descriptive for subgroups, not multiple independent confirmatory claims.

95% intervals: paired game-cluster bootstrap, 2,000 replicates, seed 2026100902. Keep every sampled observation from a resampled game together; report paired effects and cell counts. No significance-based optional stopping or outcome-dependent extra data. Re-run only for documented correctness defects. Also evaluate a fixed 2,400-position sensitivity sample (100 per cell) using equal self/opponent conditioning, rounded to the nearest 100 and clamped 600–2600, to match app configuration. Keep separate from actual-rating primary.

Opening investigation: up to 4,000 observations (500 per band/color) from eligible first ten full moves of main-split games, cap two per game/color. Select before model results. Compare copying and reversal probability to actual human frequencies, top-one/NLL, and history-induced changes. Additionally use a fixed hand-crafted legal opening challenge suite (pawn/knight moves; White and Black), clearly labeled diagnostics. Original historical bug examples may be unavailable; do not label new tests as reproductions of an unarchived incident. Openings use human-provided histories and do not establish autonomous rollout behavior.

Tactical diagnostics: preselect 384 main positions (16 per cell, deterministic seed 2026100903) plus a separate illustrative set containing the user's Qc2 and Qf1 examples. On each representative position evaluate EVERY legal move at 20,000 Stockfish nodes per child, Threads=1, Hash=32, cleared hash per candidate, no tablebases. Exact terminal handling. A preselected 48-position subset (2 per cell) repeats at 80,000 nodes per child for budget sensitivity. Engine evaluations are limited-search estimates. Record score in original mover's perspective, mate output separately; clip cp-equivalent scores to +/-1000 for expected-loss summary. Severe loss: un-clipped equivalent loss >=200 cp; report within viable positions whose best estimate >=-200 cp, and separately already-lost positions. Also exact immediate stalemate mass, any mate-in-one available, and probability of missing all immediate mates. Report probability of stalemate from positions estimated winning (best >=300 cp) separately. Nucleus p=.95 applied identically with inclusive crossing-move semantics is a secondary sensitivity, not mixed into primary. Use human played-move error indicators as a contextual comparator.

Verification/deliverables: hashes of data, checkpoint, source and scripts; raw position/history identities, all legal probabilities; coverage and parity evidence; timing; reproducible commands; HTML research report plus JSON results. Validate selected records via independent upstream PyTorch, legal replay and metric recomputation. Separate implementation correctness, human prediction, tactical risk, and full-game strength. No absolute Elo or phone-runtime claim, no claim of proven training-pipeline parity absent accessible evidence, no external posting.

Decision: support history when it improves representative human likelihood and move matching without a clear opening-copy or viable-position severe-error regression. If results conflict or tactical intervals are broad, state uncertainty and recommend further study rather than selecting an adaptive rule post hoc. These stages cannot establish full-game strength or long-run self-generated behavior.
