# Discord evidence notes

Reviewed on 2 October 2026 in the Maia Chess community. Discord links may require signing in and joining the server. The relevant channel is [general](https://discord.com/channels/1275283861890138122/1275283861890138125). Displayed dates below use the Asia/Tokyo timezone. These are paraphrases of community observations, not controlled experiments or verified deployment configuration.

- [25 September 2026, ComradeRamen](https://discord.com/channels/1275283861890138122/1275283861890138125/1552823323014995979): reports that temperature around 1 matches variation in Lichess openings. The post gives no sample size, metric, dataset or confidence interval.
- [26 September 2026, ComradeRamen](https://discord.com/channels/1275283861890138122/1275283861890138125/1553341614368493649): explicitly lists **Temperature 1, TopP 95%**. This is evidence for a recommended setting, not evidence that every Lichess Maia3 bot uses 1/1. The nearby preceding post includes an attachment without accessible explanatory text.
- [25 September 2026, Dash](https://discord.com/channels/1275283861890138122/1275283861890138125/1552790790097342547): describes Temperature 0.5 / TopP 0.9 as a personally useful compromise and links the local-stack guide, explicitly acknowledging the anecdotal nature of the advice. The following discussion explains that an opening book was used on Mac, so opening variety did not have to come entirely from Maia.
- [15 July 2026, Tdiditd](https://discord.com/channels/1275283861890138122/1275283861890138125/1526828997026255018): asks what settings the website uses, inferring 1/1 from the GitHub repository. This is a question/inference, not operator confirmation.
- [28 June 2026, coffeechessandpizza](https://discord.com/channels/1275283861890138122/1275283861890138125/1520777085084373124): reports three ten-game matches against Maia2-1100. Maia3-1600 reportedly scored 5.5/10 at 1/1, 10/10 at temperature 0, and 6/10 at temperature 0.5 / TopP 0.9. No PGNs, seeds, controlled opening protocol or runtime pins were attached in the visible post. Ten games per condition cannot support a precise Elo claim.
- [Earlier 28 June 2026 setup description](https://discord.com/channels/1275283861890138122/1275283861890138125/1520643414088224859): describes 79M on Apple MPS with `--use-uci-history`, initially 1500/1500 and no book unless supplied. The history setting is an additional reason not to treat the adjacent ten-game anecdote as a directly transferable Mobile Maia benchmark.

Search coverage: server searches for `temperature` (12 results), `TopP` (5 results), and quoted `Top P` (1 result), plus surrounding September 24–29 context. Older results about Maia 1 / LC0 are excluded from Maia3 conclusions. Search results are not an exhaustive archive, and the browser later encountered Discord's own crash/reload page.

Conclusion from discussion alone: the opening-variety claim is a reasonable testable hypothesis. The retrieved Discord material does not establish a universal 1/1 deployment configuration or a measured Elo effect. The report should combine this testimony with direct experiments and separately sourced bot-operator statements.

Additional original context verified: [18 August 2026, ComradeRamen](https://discord.com/channels/1275283861890138122/1296691291517878433/1539099187948556358) identifies the linked `maia3-79m_1800` Lichess bot as one they set up and says it plays from the probability distribution.
