# Matched-position evaluation diagnostic

This secondary study separates the effects of Temperature and TopP on the same nonopening positions. It supports interpretation of the complete-game strength experiments; it does **not** measure Elo or turn a nominal Maia rating into an achieved rating.

## Fixed cohort and evaluation

We froze the first 30 completed games from each of the three main 79M matchups. We selected positions after plies 24, 25, 40, 41, 60 and 61, deduplicated the first four FEN fields, and excluded terminal positions. This gives 453 unique positions: 227 White to move and 226 Black to move. The same positions and legal move evaluations are used for every sampler. The source snapshots and selection are archived in `source_first30_games.json` and `positions.json`.

Maia3-79M is conditioned on 1600/1600 and uses the app's repeated-current-board encoding. Temperature precedes TopP, and the nucleus includes the move crossing the threshold, matching Mobile Maia. Argmax is separate. This grid studies T in {0.5,0.9,1} and P in {0.5,0.9,0.95,1}; pairs below are (T,P).

Stockfish 18 evaluated **every legal move** using MultiPV at depth 10, one thread and 64MiB hash. The first two positions in each of the eighteen source-pair/ply strata were also evaluated at depth 14 (36 positions). All actual returned PV depths matched the requested depths. Raw records include FEN, provenance, legal logits, CP/mate scores, depth, nodes, time and PV. Stockfish move scores are from the side-to-move perspective.

Evaluation loss is `max(0, best legal move score - move score)`. We compute exact expectations from each policy distribution, rather than sampling moves. Mate scores are mapped to ±10000cp for the raw metric, so we also report each loss capped at 1000cp and probabilities of losses exceeding 100cp and 200cp. These thresholds are evaluation gaps, **not guaranteed game-losing blunders**: forfeiting a forced mate while remaining winning can create a large gap.

Means give equal weight to the eighteen source-pair/ply strata. Confidence intervals for paired policy differences resample contributing source games within each matchup, preserving their multiple sampled positions, using 2000 bootstrap replicates. The convenience cohort is generated by the policies under study; intervals are conditional on it and do not include engine error or uncertainty about all human positions.

Compared with the previous default (0.5,0.9), full policy (1,1) increased expected capped loss by **24.52cp** (paired game-cluster 95% interval 19.49–30.13cp). Its probability of an evaluation gap exceeding 100cp increased by **7.51 percentage points** (6.31–8.67); exceeding 200cp increased by **4.08 points** (3.33–4.87).

The controls interact. At T=0.5, raising P from 0.9 to 1 increased capped expected loss by 2.84cp and the >100cp probability by 0.95 points. At P=0.9, raising T from 0.5 to 1 increased those quantities by 15.62cp and 4.99 points. Raising P from 0.9 to 1 at T=1 then added 8.90cp and 2.51 points. In this fixed cohort, temperature accounts for more of the baseline-to-full difference. These are conditional diagnostic changes, not transferable Elo penalties.

P=1 retains all legal moves, averaging 30.67 here. Its entropy is only 1.91 bits at T=1: retaining a move is not the same as giving it appreciable probability or choosing uniformly.

## Depth and score sensitivity

The 36-position depth 14 check also shows higher probabilities of >100cp and >200cp evaluation gaps for full policy: differences +4.79 points (95% interval 1.90–8.49) and +3.43 points (1.70–5.21). Its capped expected loss difference is +9.01cp, with an interval spanning zero (-1.80–21.31). The raw mate-mapped expected loss difference reverses sign (-87.51cp, interval -214.68–52.82). This subset has only two positions per stratum and strongly tactical mate-sensitive positions. It does not justify a claim that every lower-temperature policy is always stronger, or that raw expected CP loss is stable with search depth.

After the main results, we specified a secondary cached-data check restricted to positions whose **best** legal root evaluation is nonmate and within inclusive [-300,+300]cp. This retains 271 of 453 positions (59.8%), with every one of the eighteen strata represented. The selected IDs and all grid profiles are in `summary_close_positions.json`. This rule is common to every policy and required no new engine evaluations. It is an exploratory robustness check because it was added after inspecting the main study, and individual alternative moves can still receive mate scores.

For that close-position subset, the previous default's capped expected loss is 52.34cp versus 74.30cp for full policy: a difference of **21.96cp** (14.38–28.89). The >100cp probabilities are 11.88% versus 20.31%, a difference of **8.43 points** (6.73–10.24); the >200cp probabilities are 4.14% versus 8.85%, a difference of **4.72 points** (3.45–6.14). Resampling discarded 96 of 2000 replicates that emptied a sparse stratum; 1904 valid replicates remain. The same qualitative threshold/capped-loss tradeoff survives this restriction.

## Reproduction and artifacts

`summary_balanced.json` is the authoritative combined-cohort output. The working study retains the superseded White-only `summary.json` and `metrics.json`; these are excluded from the publication bundle and must not be cited as final combined results. `manifest_combined.json` records the combined configuration. `verification.json` confirms 453 positions, 489 analyses, complete legal-move coverage and matching requested/actual depths. `AUDIT.md` documents the independent main-harness/app parity audit and limitations.

Use the separate `recompute_diagnostics.py` for reproduction. It validates the archived raw/position hashes and consumes the **exact 489 frozen position/depth requests in their original order**, rather than selecting new positions from whichever tournament files exist. The original two-stage cohort/evaluation scripts are retained as measured source records; their local paths and append state are not the preferred public reproduction interface.

From the research directory, install/use the Python dependencies in `strength/portable/requirements.lock`, as described in `strength/portable/README.md`. For example, after creating that environment:

```sh
strength/portable/.venv/bin/python diagnostics/recompute_diagnostics.py --cached-only --output /absolute/path/new-cached-results
```

**Cached summary rederivation requires no model file and no Stockfish executable.** It uses the archived legal logits and move evaluations, runs the preserved summaries with their output directory redirected, and writes `summary_balanced.json`, `summary_close_positions.json`, `metrics.csv` and logs only inside a new directory. The portable Python dependencies are still required because the preserved numerical modules import them. Existing output directories are rejected; published artifacts are never replaced. This mode was tested with an intentionally nonexistent model path, and its summary files were compared with the published outputs.

For fresh inference/evaluations, set the explicit model path and supply your Stockfish executable. The model must match the pinned 79M SHA-256; the model retrieval instructions are in `strength/portable/README.md`.

```sh
export MAIA79_MODEL='/absolute/path/maia3-79m.onnx'
strength/portable/.venv/bin/python diagnostics/recompute_diagnostics.py --stockfish /absolute/path/stockfish --limit 1 --output /absolute/path/new-smoke-results
strength/portable/.venv/bin/python diagnostics/recompute_diagnostics.py --stockfish /absolute/path/stockfish --output /absolute/path/new-full-results
```

`--limit 1` evaluates just the first frozen request at depth 10, checks complete legal-move coverage and actual PV depth, and labels its output as an incomplete smoke test. Omitting the limit evaluates all 489 requests, including the 36 depth 14 refinements, then derives the complete summaries. It does not change the cohort or substitute later games. The reproduction manifest records checkpoint, engine executable/hash/identity, script and helper hashes, dependencies and platform. Fresh outputs use one thread, 64MiB hash, all-legal-move MultiPV and the original depth/mate-score conventions.

Fresh results depend on the Stockfish executable/version, platform, ONNX runtime and arithmetic; preserving the requests and model bytes does not guarantee bit-identical engine scores or logits everywhere. Cached rederivation isolates the published statistical calculations from those differences. The saved one-request smoke run is in `reproduction-smoke/`; `reproduction-cached/` records the cached reproduction check. Neither is an additional strength experiment.
