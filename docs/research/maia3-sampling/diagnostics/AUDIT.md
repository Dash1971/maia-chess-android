# Independent policy and tournament audit

Inspected on 2 October 2026: `strength/mobile_policy.py`, `run_strength.py`, `verify_policy.py`, `parity.json`, and Mobile Maia stable25's `lib/src/maia.dart`. The main running harness was read only. The app checkout is commit `f659956d1c35206600cda515f462277c63e58205`; its `maia.dart` SHA-256 is `a14a8c078a3e7c04ee777dc59dce1f980a8952b987813dc28eaeb0059665fdf8`. The policy and tournament script hashes match the running study manifests: `8e3f203b9f26b3ce0a7b01b8cf22101a23cb4339306c4f4d3d8df8794cb0bc98` and `8d13ee49b92a49a5b6248a49d7940330a76dd7da8e99053a48280f63d5fe62de` respectively.

The policy implementation matches the app's relevant operations: current board repeated in eight input slots, zero final padding channel, vertical rank reflection and piece-color swap for Black, ordinary and promotion move vocabulary indices, legal-move-only normalization, Temperature clamp, and retaining the top-p threshold-crossing move. The app implementation deliberately differs from the inspected upstream sampler's exclusive cutoff. This harness correctly measures the app's policy rather than reproducing that upstream discrepancy.

`verify_policy.py` was rerun successfully. Its four fixtures cover 75 legal-move logits, including Black, differing self/opponent ratings, and Black promotion. All top moves matched; maximum absolute logit error was 0.0000228452. Synthetic checks exercise crossing inclusion and TopP zero. Model SHA-256 is `3454b03ae78baa64a87b345fdb1a457265d912caec531039b074f07eda0d8010`. This validates the selected reference cases; it is not an exhaustive equivalence proof.

The tournament fixes both rating inputs to 1600, starts each game from the standard initial position without a book, alternates colors equally within each matchup, uses explicit independent per-game random seeds, records legal moves and results durably, and records model and harness hashes. No material encoding or sampling defect was found in the inspected path.

Relevant limits:

- Python and Dart do not share identical random streams; the categorical distribution is the parity target.
- Exact equal-logit tie ordering can depend on each chess library's legal-move order. No problematic fixture tie was observed.
- `claim_draw=True` permits a claim based on an available next move, including a prospective third repetition; the app's automatic draw behavior need not have identical timing. Retain termination counts and identify this tournament rule.
- Games capped at 400 plies are recorded as draws; report their number and inspect whether that convention could materially affect comparisons.
- The entire study uses the app's repeated-current-position input. Results cannot be attributed automatically to genuine-history backends or other Maia versions.
- Each profile changes both controls simultaneously in the primary three-way tournament. The secondary fixed-position grid isolates their separate probability effects but does not yield an Elo conversion.

The secondary diagnostic selects plies 24, 25, 40, 41, 60, and 61 from the first 30 completed games per matchup, removes terminal positions and duplicates using the first four FEN fields, and applies every profile to the same selected positions. Odd plies were specified and appended from the same frozen source-game snapshot before inspecting combined results, to include Black as well as White. Equal weighting of the eighteen source-pair/ply strata balances the sources and colors despite unequal survival and deduplication counts. The evaluation cohort is generated by the compared policies, rather than independently sampled human positions.

Stockfish MultiPV evaluates every legal move at depth 10, with one thread and a 64 MiB hash. Two positions from each source-pair/ply stratum are repeated at depth 14. Centipawn losses are differences from the best Stockfish-scored move in the same position and depth, mapping mate scores to ±10000 centipawns. Report mate-related sensitivity, including losses capped at 1000cp, not just raw centipawn means. Thresholds strictly greater than 100 and 200 centipawns are reported as expected probabilities, not observed game-level blunder rates or Elo. Confidence intervals resample contributing games within each source pair, preserving each game's sampled positions, and measure paired differences on this fixed convenience cohort. They do not cover all population-selection or engine-evaluation uncertainty.

Centipawn losses in already lost positions can be large without much effect on the game's result. Mate mappings can also dominate the raw mean, and the small depth14 subset contains several sharply tactical positions. This diagnostic complements complete-game outcomes and must not replace them. Retained support counts every positive-probability legal move; moves remain weighted by Maia's probabilities, so a large support count does not imply that all those moves occur frequently.
