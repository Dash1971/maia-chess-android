# Mobile Maia 2.5.0

**More ways to make Mobile Maia your own—and understand how Maia sees the game.**

- **Light and dark themes:** choose Light, Dark or follow your system setting. Optional Android system colors and a pure-black background are also available. The original appearance remains the default.
- **Customize your board:** choose from 25 board designs and 16 piece sets. Adjust coordinates, move indicators, highlighting, animation and dragging aids.
- **Set up positions visually:** the graphical board editor supports placing, moving and removing pieces, flipping the board, and copying or sharing FEN positions.
- **See Maia’s assessment:** an optional score percentage shows how Maia expects players at the selected rating to score from a position. Compare two ratings alongside Stockfish, and tap the percentage for an explanation and our research into its reliability. Enable it under **Settings → Analysis → Show Maia win (score) %**.
- **Continue on Chessnut:** start an untimed game from an analysis position. The board’s lights guide you through arranging the pieces, and play begins when the position matches.
- **Smoother variation navigation:** inside a variation, hold Left to return to its main-line branching point. Hold Right to return there and advance one main-line move.
- **Updated defaults and clearer help:** new installations and Settings resets use Maia 1500 for play and primary analysis, and 2600 for the optional second engine. Temperature defaults to 1.0, Top-P to 0.95, and opening variety is enabled. Engine explanations are updated across all ten languages.

**Your existing settings are preserved.** The new defaults do not overwrite saved choices.

Maia’s score percentage includes draws—it is an estimate of expected points, rather than simply the chance of winning. [Read the score-reliability research](https://github.com/Dash1971/mobile-maia-research/blob/main/studies/score-reliability-2026-10-27/REPORT.md).

Mobile Maia remains free, open source and offline. Chessnut Air and Go are supported; DGT and Grand Kingdom support remain unavailable in Stable.

Android version code: **82**.

## Promotion scope and acceptance

The proposed app behavior follows Preview 2.5.0-beta.10 source
`96745a6e49c1b66962b2219ebb23e35a5a1ef1a4` and Preview main
`e1688ddb8e404716961824dfc9e51d992b238a16` (publication documentation), subject
to the final source audit. Stable keeps its unflavored
`com.dash1971.maia_chess` package, blue identity, 79M model, existing signing
identity and Stable release gates. Preview/Dev identities and the Dev 5M model
are not promoted. Existing DGT Pegasus and Square Off Grand Kingdom prototype
source remains present but blocked in Stable. Timed physical-board
games, USB and direct physical editing/input in Analysis Board remain outside
this feature.

The requester reports that the Chessnut tests passed and authorized release qualification and
publication. That is **user-reported hardware acceptance**; the preparer has not
independently tested physical Bluetooth, LEDs or a board. Archived Preview
emulator and controlled-transport qualification are separate evidence, not
substitutes for physical testing or exact-source Stable qualification.

Existing settings, games, analysis trees and sampling data are intended to be
preserved. Release qualification must verify the actual signed Stable 2.4 → 2.5 upgrade;
Preview upgrade evidence alone does not establish that result.
