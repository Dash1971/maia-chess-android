# Mobile Maia 2.2.0

Mobile Maia 2.2 makes on-device play and review more informative, more tactile,
and more resilient. The [Stable APK](https://github.com/Dash1971/maia-chess-android/releases/tag/v2.2.0)
is available on GitHub for GitHub/Obtainium installs. F-Droid follows its
separate update and reproducible-build process.

## Highlights

- **Compare two Maia ratings.** Enable a second Maia engine in Settings to see
  how two human-strength levels would play the same position. Analysis Board and
  full-game review can show Maia 1600 and Maia 2400 probabilities alongside two
  Stockfish 19 Light lines, with arrows on the board.
- **Hear and feel the game.** Optional Game sounds distinguish moves,
  captures, invalid moves, and game end. Optional haptic feedback provides touch
  cues for moves, checks, errors, and game end. Phone feedback is suppressed
  while a Chessnut board controls play; its board sounds are a separate setting.
- **Understand games faster.** Full-game review uses Fast as the new-game/reset
  default, while Balanced and Thorough remain available. Classification and
  navigation feedback are clearer. The review shows Brilliant, Good,
  Interesting, Dubious, Mistake, and Blunder annotations on the board and move
  list; the [Byrne–Fischer example](../screenshots/20261003_v0_stable_2_2_brilliant_review.png)
  shows a genuine `17...Be6!!` in the signed Stable app.
- **Find the right saved game.** Recent games now identify player side and Maia
  rating, and display the actual result (`1-0`, `0-1`, or `1/2-1/2`) or
  `Incomplete`, plus the saved date.
- **Continue from any analyzed position.** The new Continue from here dialog
  lets you choose side, Play Maia rating, and clock—including custom minutes
  and increment—for one game without overwriting saved Home choices.
- **More robust offline play.** Transactional saved-game checkpoints retain a
  previous readable copy for interrupted-write recovery. Variation recovery
  and native-engine bridge checks were strengthened; stale sound playback is
  suppressed after a game or settings change. Privacy-safe Chessnut diagnostics
  make troubleshooting easier without automatically uploading a report.
  Chessnut support remains experimental.

New and reset games default to **Temperature 1.00 / Top-P 1.00**, allowing a
wider range of Maia openings. Existing valid custom choices remain saved. The
[sampling report](../research/maia3-sampling/REPORT.md) explains the
strength-versus-variety tradeoff.

## Package and verification

The universal APK bundles the on-device Maia-3 79M model and ARMv7, ARM64,
and x86_64 libraries. It requires no account or Internet permission.

Release source: `e5c2392c4d979a47e458ba6f571c72ca60ae817b`.

Two independent unsigned builds were byte-identical. The 16-case native suite,
signed minified-APK smoke test, and three production-signed 2.1.3→2.2.0
saved-game upgrade fixtures passed. The signing certificate matches Stable
2.1.3; the signed archive passed package and privacy checks. Stable was tested
on an x86_64 emulator. Real-device ARM64 feedback came from Preview beta.9,
which contains the same promoted app changes, not from an install of this
Stable APK.
