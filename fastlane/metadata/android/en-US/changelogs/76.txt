Mobile Maia 2.1.3 updates ONNX Runtime from 1.23.2 to 1.24.3, fixing a native
inference crash on affected Android 16 devices using Snapdragon SM8850-class
processors. The fix was confirmed on a Nubia Z80 Ultra through the Preview
build before promotion to Stable. Existing features, offline behavior, saved
games, settings, supported ABIs, and reproducible F-Droid release path remain
unchanged.
