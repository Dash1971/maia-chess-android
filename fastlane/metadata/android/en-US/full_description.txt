Mobile Maia is an offline chess app for Android using Maia-3 79M and Stockfish.

Choose a Maia rating from 600 to 2600 to play against and analyze human-like moves at different playing strengths.

<b>Features</b><ul><li>Full-game analysis with evaluation graphs, accuracy scores and move annotations.</li><li>Save analysis in PGN and restore it when reopening games.</li><li>Import and export PGN collections up to 5 MiB, with duplicate checking.</li><li>Choose whether to include analysis when saving, sharing or copying PGN.</li><li>Edit positions, explore variations and continue playing from a chosen position.</li><li>Adjust clocks, move variety and timing. Use takebacks and multiple premoves.</li><li>Phone and tablet layouts, ten languages, and customizable boards and themes.</li><li>Untimed Bluetooth play with Chessnut Air, Go, Pro and Square Off Grand Kingdom.</li></ul>
Free and open source under AGPL-3.0-only. The Maia-3 79M model is included for offline use.
