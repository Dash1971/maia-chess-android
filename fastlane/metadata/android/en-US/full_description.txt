By Maia fans, for Maia fans. Mobile Maia is an offline chess app for Android using the latest Maia-3 79M engine and Stockfish.

Choose a Maia rating from 600 to 2600 to play against and analyze human-like moves at different playing strengths.

<ul><li>Full-game analysis with evaluation graphs, accuracy scores and move annotations. (including brilliant moves!!)</li><li>Save analysis in PGN and restore it when reopening games.</li><li>Import and export PGN collections, with or without analysis.</li><li>Analysis board with opening names and multiple colored arrows to easily compare Stockfish and Maia analysis.</li><li>Edit positions, explore variations and continue playing from a chosen position.</li><li>Full set of Lichess time controls or choose a custom time control.</li><li>Use takebacks, agree to draws, and stack chess.com style multiple premoves.</li><li>Phone and tablet layouts, ten languages, and customizable boards and themes.</li><li>Bluetooth eboard play with Chessnut Air, Go, Pro and Square Off Grand Kingdom.</li></ul>
Free and open source under AGPL-3.0-only. The Maia-3 79M model is included for offline use.
