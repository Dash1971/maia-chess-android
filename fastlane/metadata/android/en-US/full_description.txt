<p>Mobile Maia is a free, open-source Android chess app for playing against Maia-3, a human-like model from the University of Toronto Computational Social Science Lab, and analysing games alongside Stockfish.</p>

<p>Everything runs on your device: no account, subscription, ads, or Internet connection is needed. The Maia-3 79M model, Stockfish 19 Light, opening database, saved games, and analysis are available offline.</p>

<p>Play:</p>
<ul>
<li>Choose White, Black, or a random side and a Play Maia rating from 600 to 2600</li>
<li>Use unlimited play, clock presets, or custom minutes and increment</li>
<li>Adjust human-like move timing, Temperature, and Top-P</li>
<li>Use single or multiple premoves, with an optional 100 ms penalty</li>
<li>Take back moves, offer endgame draws, play rematches, and restore unfinished games</li>
<li>Enable optional move sounds and haptic feedback</li>
<li>Find recent games by player side, Maia rating, result, and date</li>
</ul>

<p>Explore and learn:</p>
<ul>
<li>Compare move probabilities from two Maia ratings alongside two Stockfish suggestions</li>
<li>See agreement between the engines with arrows on the board</li>
<li>Run Fast, Balanced, or Thorough full-game analysis; Fast is the default for new or reset settings</li>
<li>Review accuracy, the evaluation graph, and Brilliant, Good, Interesting, Dubious, Mistake, and Blunder annotations</li>
<li>Import FEN or PGN, edit positions, and build persistent nested variations</li>
<li>Continue from an analysed position with a choice of side, playing strength, and clock for that game, without changing Home preferences</li>
<li>Look up offline opening names and ECO codes</li>
<li>Save and share PGN with variations, annotations, and historical clocks</li>
</ul>

<p>Experimental Chessnut support includes board LEDs, sounds, battery status, reconnect recovery, and the option to finish a board game on the phone. Board play supports unlimited games from the standard starting position; USB and Analysis Board input are not supported.</p>

<p>The bundled offline model makes the universal APK approximately 435 MiB. Mobile Maia is an independent community project under AGPL-3.0-only and is not an official Maia Chess, Lichess, Stockfish, or Chessnut application.</p>
